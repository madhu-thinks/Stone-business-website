About Our Practice

Heritage in Every Stone

We combine decades of family tradition with modern precision to create temple and architectural carvings that endure for generations.

Our Story

Shree Venkateshwara Shilpa Shala began as a family stone carving studio rooted in traditional temple artistry. Over 25+ years, our founder has delivered pure white and black stone idol work, sacred temple architecture, and trusted restoration for religious authorities.

Family Legacy

Our story is built on a belief that every stone carving should preserve cultural heritage while honoring faith and form.

Pure Stone Mastery

We specialize in hand-carved god idols and temple panels made from pure white and black stone, combining traditional skill with disciplined craftsmanship.

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To craft distinguished stone works that honor traditions, elevate sacred spaces and support architectural excellence.

Vision

To be the leading stone carving partner for temples, government heritage projects, and premium architectural commissions.

Values

Respect for heritage, precision in every detail, reliability in delivery, and lasting relationships with clients.

Founder Profile

Our founder has dedicated 25+ years to temple carving, god idol sculpture and major sacred commissions across South India.
