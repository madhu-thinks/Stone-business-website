Project Questions

Answers to the most common questions about stone carving, restoration, materials and timelines.

Common Questions

What is the typical project timeline?

Most temple and architectural projects take 4–12 weeks depending on scope, stone type and installation requirements.

How much does a project cost?

Cost varies by stone selection, scale and design complexity. We provide a detailed estimate after consultation.

Which materials are best for outdoor use?

Granite and sandstone are preferred for outdoor carvings due to durability and weather resistance.

Do you offer restoration services?

Yes, we restore heritage temples and damaged stonework using traditional techniques and careful conservation practices.

Can you work on international projects?

We primarily serve local and regional projects, but can consider international work on a case-by-case basis.

What is your quality guarantee?

We stand behind our workmanship with rigorous quality checks and follow-up support to ensure long-term satisfaction.
