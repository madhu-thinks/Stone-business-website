Stone Types

We work with premium natural materials chosen for strength, color and carving suitability.

Granite

Durable, weather-resistant and ideal for temple pillars, exterior cladding, and heavy structural carvings.

  • Hardness: Very high
  • Best use: Temples, façades, sculptures
  • Color range: Dark grey, black, red

Marble

Elegant, refined and excellent for deity sculptures, interior elements and polished architectural features.

  • Hardness: Medium
  • Best use: Sculptures, halls, altars
  • Color range: White, cream, veined

Sandstone

Warm, natural texture with strong cultural appeal for heritage projects and ornamental stonework.

  • Hardness: Medium
  • Best use: Carving details, facades, pathways
  • Color range: Beige, warm brown

Limestone

Soft and workable for detailed relief work, interior installations and decorative stone panels.

  • Hardness: Medium-low
  • Best use: Ornamental carving, interior details
  • Color range: Cream, light grey
